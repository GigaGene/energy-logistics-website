Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Energy Logistics LLC collects only the information needed to respond to shipper, broker, driver, owner operator, and business inquiries.

Information We Collect

  • Contact details submitted through website forms, including name, company, email, phone, state, and message details.
  • Freight request details such as origin, destination, commodity, equipment needs, and scheduling notes.
  • Driver or owner operator information submitted for recruiting and program review.
  • Broker verification details and secure document links submitted for carrier packet requests.
  • Basic security data such as IP address hashes, timestamps, spam indicators, and failed submission logs.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to freight, broker, dispatch, recruiting, and owner operator requests.
  • To verify broker access before releasing carrier packet materials.
  • To protect forms from spam, abuse, fraud, and unsafe file uploads.
  • To maintain operational records, security logs, and backup records where required.

Documents and Sensitive Information

Driver documents, broker documents, and private business materials are not published on the public website. Uploaded files are restricted to safe document formats and should be reviewed through secure internal systems only. Telegram and email notifications should contain summaries only, not full sensitive documents.

Cookies and Analytics

The website uses essential browser storage for form safety and cookie notice preferences. If analytics pixels are enabled, analytics keys must be stored outside frontend source code where possible and visitors should be shown the cookie notice.

Sharing

Information may be shared internally with dispatch, HR, accounting, or administration based on the request. We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers used for secure hosting, email delivery, analytics, backup, or operational systems.

Security

We use HTTPS, form validation, spam controls, security headers, restricted uploads, role-based access planning, and operational logging to protect business and personal data. No website can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to dispatch@energylog.org.
