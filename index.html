About Energy Logistics LLC

Washington-Based Freight Transportation Across the United States.

Energy Logistics LLC is a Washington-based transportation company providing refrigerated, frozen, dry goods, and general freight transportation across the United States.

We serve brokers, shippers, warehouses, distributors, manufacturers, and businesses that need dependable freight movement with professional communication and reliable service.

Our company is built around safety, respect, communication, and long-term partnerships. Whether we are working with a broker, shipper, customer, CDL driver, or owner-operator, our goal is to operate with responsibility and professionalism.
